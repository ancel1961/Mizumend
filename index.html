                              What happened at the gate

                              Someone turned the sluice by hand that night, the ninth of the rains, with the river at the door. The bell on the hill rang too late. The water went through the mill pond instead of the village below, and the village was spared.

                              So that is what happened at the gate. But the notes in the bottles are still written to a small one. Whose?
